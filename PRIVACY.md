# Privacy Policy

Scattered Notes for Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad and Android doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

## Your notes

Your notes are plain Markdown files in a folder you choose: on your device, in your iCloud Drive, or in a folder another sync app (such as Dropbox, OneDrive or Syncthing) keeps in step. Scattered Notes reads and writes only that folder. When the folder is in iCloud Drive or another sync service, that service copies the files under your own account and its own privacy policy. Scattered Notes never sends your notes anywhere itself.

## Update checks

The Mac and Windows apps, and the Android app downloaded from GitHub, ask GitHub once a day whether a new version is out, and download it when you choose to install it. Like any web request, this tells GitHub your IP address; GitHub's privacy statement applies. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings on the Mac and on Windows. The iPhone and iPad app (from TestFlight or the App Store) and the Android app from Google Play make no such requests: Apple and Google deliver their updates.
